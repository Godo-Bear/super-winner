    ❓ How to play

    • WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint · Mouse orbit camera (click to lock, Esc to release)
    • Every 15s a random survivor becomes the Writer. Their word becomes a hazard. Hazards stack!
    • Every hazard warns you first: red circles, shadows, sirens and wind-ups. Something always lets you survive.
    • ☢️ Nukes: hide in the hut. 🌊 Tsunamis: climb the hill. 🔴 Lasers: jump.
    • Unknown words create a Mystery Blob. The same word always makes the same creature.
    • Coins: +5 per hazard survived, +10 per kill from your word, +50 for winning.
